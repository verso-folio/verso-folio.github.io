And when he again returned to his writing desk,\
His mind blossomed and the thoughts clustered in the boughs\
Like acorns in a wise old tree that had stood for generations,\
Falling once they grew heavy on creaking neurons;\
It gathered obsessions, birds' nests in its high folds,\
And every day the sky filled with the chirping of their squabbling songs.
